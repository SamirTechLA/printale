Frequently asked questions

Personalized, but parent-led.

What makes a PrinTale story personalized?

The hero, story idea, language, and illustration direction can reflect the child and the adventure they imagine.

Are real child photos placed in the finished book?

No. If a parent chooses to make a cartoon preview, the original photo is not used in the finished storybook.

Who reviews the book?

A parent reviews the words and illustrations before a book is approved for any print workflow.

Can the story be made in more than one language?

PrinTale supports story and site options in English, Portuguese, German, Spanish, and Italian.
